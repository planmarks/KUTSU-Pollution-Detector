Kutsu Setup

Connect a Kutsu Sense device and get it on your network.

This browser can’t talk to hardware. Open Kutsu Setup in desktop Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (v89+). Web Bluetooth / Web Serial aren’t available in Firefox or Safari.

Connect your device

Power the device on and keep it nearby. Bluetooth is wireless; USB needs the cable (and works even before WiFi exists).

Need to flash or update firmware? Open Kutsu Flasher ↗ — installs the latest firmware over USB in your browser.
