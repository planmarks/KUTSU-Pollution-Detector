# KUTSU — Product Description

[← KUTSU 1.0](README.md)

KUTSU is an environmental monitoring and awareness device. It focuses on harmful and volatile
organic compounds carried through the air.

## Configurations

KUTSU comes in three configurations:

1. **Open-source development platform.** Encourages enthusiasts to explore environmental
   preservation and civic projects.
2. **Handheld.** For on-site, situational air-quality assessment.
3. **Stationary.** For long-term air-quality monitoring across large industrial sites, with a
   wider range of measurable compounds.

### KUTSU Handheld

A battery-powered unit with a display-led interface, buzzer, warning LED and laser marker, for
spot checks wherever you carry it. See the [Handheld DIY build](../KUTSU%20DIY/KUTSU%20Handheld/README.md)
for the earlier dedicated design.

### KUTSU Stationary

A 5 V-powered unit with an active fan and the optional MQ-2 gas/smoke sensor. Several units
link into a self-hosted Wi-Fi dashboard that covers a whole site. See the
[Stationary DIY build](../KUTSU%20DIY/KUTSU%20Stationary/README.md) for the earlier dedicated
design.
